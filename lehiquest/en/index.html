LehiQuest

A free, unofficial app to study the Book of Mormon: for every book, a card with the story, a timeline, key verses and a notebook; a chapter-by-chapter guide with in-depth topics; one chapter a day; and games on three levels, also to play as a family.

  • Free, with no advertising and no purchases.
  • In English and Italian.
  • Works without an account: your progress stays on your phone.
  • The text of the scriptures opens in the Gospel Library app or on the website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

LehiQuest is not an app of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The study texts are written by us and summarize the scriptures in our own words.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The account is optional and only lets you find your notes and progress on another phone or tablet. You can create one from the age of 14, with a code sent by email, with Google or, on iPhone, with Apple.

I don't receive the code by email

Check your spam or junk folder: the message comes from “LehiQuest” (avvisi@ffhorizon.studio). The code is valid for one hour; if it has expired, ask for a new one. If you already have a code, tap “I already have a code” in the app.

I have a new phone

Install LehiQuest on the new phone, tap “Sign in” on the books screen and sign in the same way as before (same email, same Google or Apple account): your progress downloads by itself.

Verses open in the browser instead of the Gospel Library

If the Gospel Library app is installed on your phone, references open there; otherwise the website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints opens in the browser.

How do I unlock the game levels?

Each game has three levels: the next one opens with at least 2 stars in the one before. You can play again whenever you like: only your best result counts.

How do I switch between English and Italian?

The app starts in the language of your phone (English if it is neither English nor Italian). To change it, tap “IT/EN” at the top of the books screen.
