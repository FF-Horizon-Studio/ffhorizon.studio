Delete your account

You can delete your LehiQuest account and all the progress saved online at any time. There are two ways.

1. From the app (the quickest way)

  1. Open LehiQuest and tap “Synced” at the top of the books screen: the Account page opens.
  2. At the bottom, in the “Delete account” box, tap “Delete my account”.
  3. Confirm with “Yes, delete”.

The account and the progress saved online are deleted immediately, and this cannot be undone. If you sign in with Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple too: this disconnects LehiQuest from your Apple Account.

2. Write to us

Send an email to hello@ffhorizon.studio with the subject “LehiQuest account deletion”, from the same email address as your account. If you sign in with Apple and chose “Hide My Email”, write to us from the address that Apple forwards to yours, or tell us how you sign in.

We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your account: email address, sign-in method and, if you used Google, the profile name and picture received from Google;
  • the date on which you consented to the privacy policy;
  • all the progress saved online: notebook notes and ratings, answers and goals in “Today”, books studied, pages viewed, chapters read and done, game stars.

What remains

The progress saved on your phone stays there, because it is yours and we don't receive it: if you want to delete that too, uninstall the app.

If you used Google to sign in, you can also remove LehiQuest from the apps connected to your Google Account, in Google's security settings.

The provider's backups are overwritten automatically according to its technical schedules. For details, read the privacy policy.

Deleting only some data, without deleting the account

  • Notebook notes: delete them in the app, on the Notebook page of the book. The change is also saved online.
  • Answers and goals in “Today”: delete them in the app, in the chapter of the daily study where you wrote them. This change is also saved online.
  • Any other data: write to hello@ffhorizon.studio with the subject “LehiQuest data deletion”, from your account's email address, saying which data you want to delete. We do it within 30 days and confirm by email.
