Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This is a translation. If there are any differences, the Italian version prevails.

LehiQuest is a free, unofficial app to study the Book of Mormon. It is not an app of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, which does not manage it and does not receive the data collected by the app.

In short

  • Without an account, your data (notebook notes, books studied, game stars) stays only on your phone: we don't receive it.
  • The account is optional and only lets you find your progress on more than one device. You can create one from the age of 14, with your explicit consent.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics, no sale of data.
  • With an account, the data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • No other user sees your data: the app has no public profiles, chats or leaderboards.
  • You can delete your account and all the data saved online directly from the app.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Francesco Foresta (FF Horizon Studio), who develops and manages the app. For any request, write to hello@ffhorizon.studio.

2. Without an account: everything stays on your phone

You can use the whole app without an account. Your notebook notes, the answers and goals you write in “Today”, the books marked as studied, the pages viewed, the chapters read and the game stars are saved only in your phone's memory: they don't reach us or any provider. If you uninstall the app, they are deleted.

Reports. With “Report” you can send us, with or without an account, a mistake in the content, a problem with the app or a suggestion. We receive by email your message, the place in the app you are writing from (for example the book, the page or the quiz question), the email address to reply to if you enter it (with an account, the account's address) and some technical data that you see in the form before sending: app version, phone system and model, screen size and language. The report passes through our server (Supabase) without being stored there and reaches our mailbox; on the server only the time of sending is kept, with no data about you, to limit abuse. Write only what the report needs.

3. With an account: what data we process

  • Account data: your email address and the way you sign in (email with a code, Google or Apple). There are no passwords: for email we send you a code each time.
    • If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your email and, as its sign-in service provides, your profile name and picture, which remain recorded with the account. The app uses only the email.
    • If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us your email: your real one or, if you choose “Hide My Email”, an anonymous address created by Apple.
  • Your progress: the notes you write in the notebook of each book and the two ratings from 1 to 5, the books marked as studied, the pages viewed, the chapters read, your best game stars, the answers and goals you write in the daily study “Today”, the chapter you have reached and the ones already done, each with the date of its last change. They let you find them on another phone or tablet.
  • Technical data: the date on which you gave your consent to this policy and the access data that our provider records for security (for example IP address and time of the requests).

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone, and does not use analytics or advertising tools. The “Today” reminder, if you turn it on, is a notification scheduled on your phone: the time you choose does not reach us.

4. A special category of data: your religious beliefs

The notes you write in the notebook and your answers in “Today” may reveal your religious beliefs, which under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are a special category of data (Art. 9). For this reason we save them online only with your explicit consent, which the app asks for before creating the account. Write in the notebook only what you want to keep.

5. Why we use the data and on what legal basis

  • Saving and syncing your progress across your devices. Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) of the GDPR).
  • Security: protecting the service from unauthorized access and abuse, with the technical access data. Legal basis: the legitimate interest in keeping the app secure (Art. 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).
  • Support: answering your requests and the reports you send us from the app, and correcting the mistakes you point out. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) of the GDPR) and, for requests about your rights, a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) of the GDPR).

We make no automated decisions about you and create no profiles.

6. Who can see your data

Only you can see your progress: the database lets each account read and change only its own data. The app manager (Francesco Foresta) can access the data only when needed for support or security. The data is not sold or given to anyone.

7. The providers who help us

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd, Singapore, with Supabase, Inc., United States), data processor: database, account sign-in and sending of reports. The data is stored on servers in the European Union, in Frankfurt (Germany).
  • Hostinger (Hostinger International Ltd., European Union), data processor: the mailbox that sends the sign-in codes and receives the messages you write to us.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them: they manage the sign-in as independent controllers, according to Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy. If you delete your account after using Apple, the app asks Apple to disconnect LehiQuest from your Apple Account.

Text of the scriptures: when you tap a reference, the passage opens in the Gospel Library app or on the website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, which process it according to their own privacy policy. The app only passes the address of the passage.

This website (privacy policy and support pages) is hosted by GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States), which records visitors' IP addresses for its own security as an independent controller, according to its privacy statement. The site uses no cookies or analytics.

Transfers outside the European Union: Supabase Pte. Ltd (Singapore) and Supabase, Inc. (United States) may access the data, which remains in Frankfurt, only for management and support, under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses included in the contract with Supabase. You can ask for a copy of the safeguards at hello@ffhorizon.studio.

8. How long we keep the data

  • Account and progress saved online: until you delete the account. When you delete it, the account and all the progress saved online are deleted immediately.
  • Data on your phone: it stays as long as you keep it or until you uninstall the app, even after deleting the account or signing out.
  • Messages you write to us for support and reports from the app: deleted 12 months after the request is closed; requests about your rights 24 months after, so that we can show we answered.
  • Technical access data and backups are deleted automatically by the providers, according to their technical schedules.

9. Young people

You can create an account from the age of 14, the age from which in Italy you can give consent on your own to online services. Anyone under 14 can use the whole app without an account: the data stays on the phone. The family mode of the games is designed for parents who play with their children on their own phone.

10. Your rights

At any time you can ask to:

  • know what data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict its use or object to the processing;
  • receive it in a machine-readable format (portability);
  • withdraw your consent: withdrawal does not make unlawful what was done before, but without consent you will no longer be able to use the account.

You can delete your account, and with it withdraw your consent, directly from the app (see how to delete your account). For anything else, write to hello@ffhorizon.studio. We answer within one month.

Right to object. You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest (security), by writing to hello@ffhorizon.studio (Art. 21 of the GDPR).

If you believe your data is not being processed correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (the Italian Data Protection Authority).

11. How we protect the data

  • All communications between the app and the servers are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • There are no passwords to keep: you sign in with a code sent by email, with Google or with Apple.
  • The database applies access rules so that each account sees only its own data.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we publish the new version here with the date of the update. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app.
